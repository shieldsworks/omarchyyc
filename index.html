About

Terminal green. Salt water.

Omarchy Yacht Club brings together Omarchy users who sail — racing, cruising, or just dreaming at the dock. We keep the same sharp, dark, terminal-like feel as Omarchy itself.

Omarchy Linux

Built around the Omarchy desktop and community. The club uses Omarchy brand marks with permission from the public brand kit; the project itself remains separate.

Open-source sailing tools

Home to the omahoy suite — open-source apps for sailors. Expect pointers here as those projects ship and mature.

In person & online

Meetups, photo drops, and merch when they are ready. No invented schedules — we only list what is real.
